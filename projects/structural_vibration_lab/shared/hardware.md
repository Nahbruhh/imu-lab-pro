# Shared hardware record

Every experiment must record:

- Sensor model, serial or asset identifier, and firmware revision
- Acquisition device and connection method
- Sensor orientation and mounting method
- Cable routing and any added mass
- Excitation device and its measurement method, if applicable
- Specimen identifiers and photographs
- Ambient conditions relevant to the measurement

Do not use a nominal component value when the experiment depends on a measured
or traceable value.
