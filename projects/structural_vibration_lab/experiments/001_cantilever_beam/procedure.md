# Experimental procedure

1. Identify the specimen and record environmental conditions.
2. Measure geometry, mass, sensor position, and mounting details.
3. Apply the approved sensor calibration and acquisition configuration.
4. Verify stationary noise, timestamps, sequence continuity, and clipping.
5. Apply the documented excitation without changing the boundary condition.
6. Record the required repetitions without selecting results by appearance.
7. Preserve raw captures and processing configuration.
8. Analyze repeatability before comparing with theory or FEA.

Numerical acceptance values must be defined before collecting the validation
dataset.
