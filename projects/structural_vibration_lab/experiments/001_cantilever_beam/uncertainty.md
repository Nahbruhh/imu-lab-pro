# Uncertainty plan

Consider at minimum:

- Beam dimensions, density, and elastic modulus
- Effective free length and clamp compliance
- Sensor and mounting mass
- Sensor calibration, noise, alignment, and bandwidth
- Sampling cadence and finite record length
- Excitation and response repeatability
- Frequency estimator and damping-fit sensitivity
- FEA mesh, boundary condition, and material assumptions

Report influential inputs and propagate uncertainty using a documented method.
Do not report more numerical precision than the evidence supports.
