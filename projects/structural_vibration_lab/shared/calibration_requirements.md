# Calibration requirements

Before structural testing, retain evidence for:

- Accelerometer bias, scale, and six-position residuals
- Gyroscope bias and stationary noise when gyro channels are used
- Selected range, sample cadence, and digital filter
- Sequence continuity, timestamp behavior, and clipping
- Calibration date, procedure version, and sensor identity

A calibration is valid only for the documented sensor, mounting assumptions,
configuration, and acceptance criteria.
