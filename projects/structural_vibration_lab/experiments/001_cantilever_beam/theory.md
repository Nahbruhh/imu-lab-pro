# Analytical model

The initial analytical baseline will use Euler–Bernoulli cantilever-beam
theory. The report must state the assumptions behind that model, including
slenderness, linear elasticity, small deflection, uniform section and
material, and an ideal fixed boundary.

Inputs must come from `experiment.yaml` or a traceable referenced source.
Differences caused by clamp compliance, sensor mass, shear deformation,
rotary inertia, or nonuniform geometry must be assessed rather than hidden by
parameter tuning.
