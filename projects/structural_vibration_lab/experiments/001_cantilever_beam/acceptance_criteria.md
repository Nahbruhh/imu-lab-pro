# Acceptance criteria

Numerical limits are intentionally unset until the measurement capability and
expected uncertainties are reviewed. Before formal data collection, define:

- Allowed clipping and sequence-loss limits
- Sample-cadence and timestamp tolerances
- Minimum usable record duration and frequency resolution
- Repeatability limits across nominally identical tests
- Peak-identification and damping-fit quality requirements
- Acceptable analytical/FEA/experimental disagreement
- Required metadata and retained evidence

Criteria must be defined before examining the final validation results.
